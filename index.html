What We Do

Commercial Expertise,
Delivered Precisely

From full-floor office fitouts to bespoke carpentry — we bring skill, precision and professionalism to every project.

01
Office Fitouts

Our signature specialty. Full commercial fitouts including frameless glass partitions, feature walls, cabinetry and all carpentry elements — delivered on time and on spec.

02
Glass Partitioning

Sleek black-framed and frameless glass partition systems for modern workplaces. From single offices to full open-plan transformations.

03
Commercial Carpentry

Custom joinery, built-in cabinetry, feature ceiling systems and all structural carpentry. Precision work that stands out and stands the test of time.

04
Tenant Improvements

Transforming leased commercial spaces with expert fitout work. We liaise with building managers and work around business hours to minimise disruption.

05
Residential Building

Extensions, renovations and new builds handled with the same commercial-grade professionalism. Quality residential work across Melbourne.

06
Project Management

Full project coordination from initial plans through to handover. We manage trades, timelines and quality control so your fitout runs smoothly.

About DKC

Craftsmen Who
Know the Trade

DKC Building & Carpentry is a Melbourne-based building and carpentry company with a reputation built on quality work, reliable delivery and honest communication. Based in Patterson Lakes, we operate across all of Victoria.

Office fitouts are where we excel — the technical complexity of glass partitioning systems, the precision of custom joinery, the coordination required to transform a live commercial floor. We've done it hundreds of times.

  • Licensed builder & registered carpenter
  • Commercial & residential projects
  • On-time, on-budget delivery
  • Fully insured & compliant
